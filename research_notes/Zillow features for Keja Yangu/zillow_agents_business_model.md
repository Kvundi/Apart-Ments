# Zillow: Agent-Facing Products and Business Model (2025-2026)

Research date: 2026-11-05. Figures are dated. Primary sources (SEC filings, Zillow press releases) are preferred; trade press and third-party sources are labelled.

## Premier Agent / Zillow Preferred: how it works, pricing, lead routing, agent tools, revenue

### Takeaway
Zillow earns most of its money by selling buyer and seller "connections" (leads) to agents. It uses two pricing models. The legacy model is "market-based pricing," where agents buy a share of voice in a ZIP code. The newer model is pay-at-close: Flex, renamed Zillow Preferred, where agents pay nothing upfront and hand Zillow a referral fee when the deal closes, about 15-40% of their commission (40% on seller leads). Zillow wraps this in agent software (Follow Up Boss CRM, ShowingTime, dotloop, Showcase) and is pushing connections into "Enhanced Markets," which bundle agent, financing and touring. Zillow stopped reporting Premier Agent revenue separately; it is now inside "Residential."

### Cited Findings
- **Two pricing models (FY2025 10-K):**
  - Market-based pricing is "a share of the total advertising purchased in a particular zip code."
  - Under subscription-based pricing, "connections are distributed to partners in proportion to their share of voice."
  - Source: [Zillow FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1617640/000161764026000015/z-20251231.htm)
- **Zillow Preferred (10-K):** an invite-only, pay-when-you-close program for top agent teams, described as "the next chapter" of Flex. Agents get leads and pay a performance fee only when a lead closes. Zillow expects Preferred to grow as Enhanced Markets expand. — [Zillow FY2025 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1617640/000161764026000015/z-20251231.htm)
- **Rename date unclear:** third-party sources disagree on when Flex became Zillow Preferred. One says 15 October 2025; another says 2026. — [The Close: Zillow Preferred](https://theclose.com/zillow-preferred/); [Zillow Preferred FAQ](https://www.zillow.com/preferred/faq/)
- **Referral fees:**
  - Seller connections: 40% in all markets, which Zillow can change with 15 days' notice. — [Zillow Flex pricing page](https://www.zillow.com/premier-agent/flex-pricing) (403 on direct fetch; figure from search snippet)
  - Buyer connections: third parties report 15-40%, depending on ZIP code, sale price and the date the connection was delivered. — [The Close: Is Zillow Flex worth it](https://theclose.com/zillow-flex/); [Zillow Flex FAQ](https://www.zillow.com/z/flex-performance-terms/flex-faq)
  - The Flex fee cap rose to 40% in October 2023. — [Mike DelPrete, "Zillow Flex Fee Rises to 40 Percent" (Oct 12, 2023)](https://www.mikedp.com/articles/2023/10/12/zillow-flex-fee-rises-to-40-percent)
- **Class action:** a suit targets the Flex and Premier Agent programs, alleging the referral fee is not disclosed to consumers. Zillow says these are business-to-business referral fees consistent with industry practice. — [HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/zillow-lawsuit-preferred-agent/); [nowbam](https://nowbam.com/?p=135124)
- **Zillow Preview:** listing agents can now earn 10% under a "Zillow Preview" arrangement (third-party explainer; verify). — [Effective Agents](https://www.effectiveagents.com/resources/zillow-preview-explained-how-listing-agents-now-earn-10)
- **Enhanced Markets** (bundled experience: Preferred agents plus Zillow Home Loans/BuyAbility plus touring):
  - Q3 2025: 34% of agent-consumer connections, up from 27% in Q2. Mid-term target is at least 75%. — [Q3 2025 earnings call (Motley Fool)](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2025/10/30/zillow-z-q3-2025-earnings-call-transcript)
  - Q4 2025: 44%, up from 21% a year earlier. — [Q4 2025 shareholder letter (MarketScreener)](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/zillow-fourth-quarter-2025-shareholder-letter-ce7e5adcdf8bf221)
  - The 10-K says Zillow "exited 2025 at over 40%." — [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1617640/000161764026000015/z-20251231.htm)
- **Follow Up Boss (CRM):**
  - Acquisition: agreement signed Oct 28, 2023, for $400M cash plus up to $100M in earnouts over 3 years. Closed Dec 8, 2023 (2023 annual report); one deal database says Nov 1. Kept as an independent brand. — [Zillow 8-K exhibit](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1617640/000161764023000108/exhibit993.htm); [Zillow 2023 ARS](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1617640/000161764024000063/a2023ars.pdf); [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2023/zillow-acquiring-follow-up-boss-for-up-to-500m-in-quest-for-housing-super-app/)
  - Features (10-K): a hub to organize customers, close deals and build teams, with AI smart-message suggestions, client insights, call summaries and intelligent lead routing. — [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1617640/000161764026000015/z-20251231.htm)
  - Q4 2025 letter: agents can generate pre-approval letters inside Follow Up Boss, which is integrated with BuyAbility and Zillow Home Loans. Agents sent more than 7M AI smart messages in 2025. — [Q4 2025 letter (MarketScreener)](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/zillow-fourth-quarter-2025-shareholder-letter-ce7e5adcdf8bf221)
- **Zillow Workspace and Zillow Pro (10-K):**
  - Workspace includes ShowingTime (tour scheduling), Zillow Showcase and dotloop (transaction management).
  - Zillow Pro is a membership bundling the tools into an AI-powered suite.
  - The 10-K excerpt did not use the name "ShowingTime+."
  - Source: [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1617640/000161764026000015/z-20251231.htm)
- **ShowingTime:** acquired in 2021; its revenue appears in Other IMT revenue from Q4 2021. — [FY2022 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1617640/000161764023000010/z-20221231.htm)
- **Premier Agent revenue history:**
  - $604.3M (2016), $761.6M (2017), $898.3M (2018). — [FY2018 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1617640/000161764019000005/q42018form10-kdoc.htm)
  - 2021: up $349M (+33%). 2022: down $105M (-8%). Implied about $1.4B in 2021 and about $1.3B in 2022 (derived, not reported). — [FY2022 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1617640/000161764023000010/z-20221231.htm)
  - In early 2023, Premier Agent revenue was still down 16% from the early-2022 peak. — [Mike DelPrete, "Zillow 3.0: Is It Working?"](https://www.mikedp.com/articles/2023/5/10/zillow-30-is-it-working-xrbnm)
- **Residential revenue (2025, 2026):** in 2025 and 2026 reporting, Premier Agent sits inside "Residential," with no separate line.
  - FY2025: Residential was $1,704M, up 7%. Growth was attributed to "agent and software offerings and the New Construction marketplace." — [Q4 2025 press release](https://zillowgroup.mediaroom.com/2026-02-10-Zillow-Group-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2025-Financial-Results)
  - Q3 2026: Residential was $419M, down 4% YoY, blamed on high mortgage rates pressuring transaction volume. — [Q3 2026 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001617640/000161764026000068/q32026991.htm)

### Inferences
- Moving from a share-of-voice subscription to pay-at-close shifts Zillow's risk toward performance and ties its revenue to transaction value. That only works with (a) good lead-to-close tracking and (b) agent partners who can be audited.
- For a small Kenyan portal, the advertising/share-of-voice model is simpler to start with. Pay-at-close needs transaction verification, which is harder without MLS or county records.
- Zillow bought its CRM (Follow Up Boss) to own the agent workflow and to measure lead conversion. A lightweight built-in lead inbox and CRM is a cheap version of this.

### Gaps
- No primary source found for "speed-to-lead" rules, meaning the response-time requirements for Preferred/Flex agents and how connections are routed by performance score. These live in Zillow's agent-facing performance terms (flex-faq), which returned 403. HousingWire and Inman have covered this, but I did not verify it.
- No current Premier Agent dollar figure for 2023-2025, because it is no longer disclosed.
- Exact date of the Flex-to-Zillow Preferred rename is unresolved.

## Agent profiles, reviews, finder, teams and brokerages

### Takeaway
Zillow agent profiles carry star ratings and reviews from verified clients, a sales history that Zillow builds automatically from listings and county records, current listings, and contact options. Agents cannot delete individual reviews; they can only respond or flag them. I found little current, primary-source detail on newer profile features such as response time or specialties.

### Cited Findings
- **Who can review:** only buyers and sellers who communicated or worked with the agent. Reviewers must register and say whether the agent helped them buy, sell, both, or neither. Zillow says all reviews are verified to prevent fakes and spam, and processing takes a few days. — [Zillow Agent Reviews & Ratings FAQ](https://www.zillow.com/premieragent/agent-reviews-and-ratings-faq)
- **What a review shows:** star rating, written feedback, transaction type, city/ZIP and date. Reviews cannot be individually deleted; agents can respond or flag a review for re-moderation. The only way to remove all reviews is to delete the profile. — [Zillow FAQ](https://www.zillow.com/premieragent/agent-reviews-and-ratings-faq); [Rick Janson blog (commercial, lower weight)](https://www.rickjanson.com/blog/which-real-estate-agent-has-the-best-reviews-in-denver/)
- **Past sales:** Zillow adds past sales to profiles automatically by cross-checking the agent's listings with county transaction records. Agents can flag inaccurate sales. — [Inman, 2013](https://inman.com/2013/11/22/zillow-adds-transaction-histories-to-agent-profiles)
- **Comparison study (2020):** Zillow's agent profiles were rated the most useful versus Realtor.com, HomeLight, Facebook and Yelp. Most reviews are solicited by agents themselves. — [Inman 2020](https://www.inman.com/2020/07/13/zillows-agent-profiles-are-the-most-useful-according-to-new-study/)
- **Reviews off Zillow:** reviews can be displayed on brokerage partners' sites through Zillow Pro for Brokers. — [Zillow Premier Agent](https://www.zillow.com/premier-agent/reviews-zillow-pro-for-brokers)
- **History:** agent ratings launched in December 2010. Renters and landlords were later allowed to review agents. — [Inman 2010](https://www.inman.com/2010/12/02/zillow-launches-agent-ratings/); [Zillow Group news](https://www.zillowgroup.com/news/zillow-logs-22000-agent-reviews-adds-ability-for-renters-and-landlords-to-review)

### Inferences
- The most defensible trust feature is verified transaction history built from authoritative data. In Kenya, with no public sales registry feed, a substitute is portal-recorded deals (the agent marks a listing "sold/let" and the client confirms) plus reviews gated to users who contacted the agent through the portal.
- Gating reviews to people who actually contacted the agent is cheap to build and copies Zillow's "communicated or worked with" rule.

### Gaps
- No primary source for the current profile fields: response-time badge, specialties, languages, team pages, brokerage pages. Agent finder/search UI details were also not verified.
- Current review verification mechanics (for example, transaction matching) are not documented beyond "verified by Zillow."

## Listing standards controversies (2025-2026): Listing Access Standards, Compass, NAR Clear Cooperation

### Takeaway
In 2025 Zillow used its traffic dominance to fight private and "pocket" listings. Its Listing Access Standards (LAS) ban from Zillow and Trulia any listing that is publicly marketed but not put into the MLS within one business day. Compass sued, lost its preliminary injunction (Feb 6, 2026) and dropped the suit (Mar 18, 2026) a day after Zillow loosened the rules and launched "Zillow Preview" pre-marketing with large brokerages. The lesson: listing supply is a strategic battleground, and a portal with traffic can set terms for brokers.

### Cited Findings
- **The rule:** listings not in the MLS or IDX within one business day of public marketing are barred. Office exclusives are allowed with a seller disclosure, shared only within one brokerage or one-to-one. MLS coming-soon and delayed-marketing listings are allowed. New construction marketed by builders, rentals and FSBO are exempt. — [Zillow Group: new Listing Access Standards](https://www.zillowgroup.com/news/zillows-new-listing-access-standards); [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/real-estate/real-estate-giants-crack-down-exclusive-off-market-home-listings-major-shift-buyers)
- **Enforcement:** warnings from May 28, 2025, enforcement from June 30, 2025. Two warnings, then the third non-compliant listing is blocked for the life of the listing agreement, including on Trulia. — [Inman, May 20, 2025](https://news.inman.com/2025/05/20/zillow-provides-clarity-on-private-listings-rule-and-when-it-rolls-out/); [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/real-estate/real-estate-giants-crack-down-exclusive-off-market-home-listings-major-shift-buyers)
- **NAR and Redfin:** NAR's delayed-marketing option (part of its 2025 Clear Cooperation changes) was slated for September 2025. Redfin's similar policy was to begin in September. — [Fox Business](https://www.foxbusiness.com/real-estate/real-estate-giants-crack-down-exclusive-off-market-home-listings-major-shift-buyers)
- **Compass ruling:** Compass sued in June 2025. On Feb 6, 2026, Judge Jeannette Vargas (SDNY) denied the preliminary injunction, finding no likelihood of success on the merits. — [NAR](https://www.nar.realtor/news/real-estate-news/law-and-ethics/judge-rejects-compasss-request-to-block-zillows-private-listing-rule); [Inman](https://www.inman.com/2026/02/06/judge-denies-compass-request-to-stop-zillows-private-listing-policy/); [HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/judge-denies-compass-injunction/)
- **Zillow loosens the rule (Mar 17, 2026):** Zillow said it would no longer ban listings advertised on a "public-facing website, mobile app, or internet real estate portal." It launched Zillow Preview pre-marketing with brokerages including Keller Williams and RE/MAX. — [Real Estate News](https://www.realestatenews.com/2026/03/18/compass-drops-lawsuit-after-zillow-embraces-pre-marketing); [The Real Deal](https://therealdeal.com/national/2026/03/18/compass-dismisses-lawsuit-against-zillow)
- **Compass drops the suit (Mar 18, 2026):** dismissed without prejudice. Zillow says its standards "remain fully in effect" and that Compass Private Exclusives remain excluded. Compass also syndicates "Coming Soon" listings to Redfin. — [The Mortgage Point](https://themortgagepoint.com/2026/03/20/compass-announces-dismissal-of-lawsuit-following-federal-court-ruling-of-zillow-ban/); [Real Estate News](https://www.realestatenews.com/2026/03/18/compass-drops-lawsuit-after-zillow-embraces-pre-marketing)
- **Unconfirmed claim:** one search result said Zillow "dropped" the ban after Compass's dismissal. Zillow's own statements contradict this. — [Mondaq law-firm analysis](https://webiis08.mondaq.com/unitedstates/real-estate/1768524/compass-v-zillow-update-lawsuit-dismissed-alliance-created)

### Inferences
- Zillow's supply comes mostly from MLS/IDX feeds, so it cares about keeping listings flowing to the MLS and away from brokerage-exclusive networks. A Kenyan portal has no MLS. Supply has to come directly from agents, developers and landlords, and anti-exclusive rules are not enforceable until the portal has dominant traffic.
- A practical takeaway is to reward listing freshness and verified, publicly available listings rather than ban anything. Zillow Preview shows that even Zillow found a "pre-market" product was more workable than pure bans.

### Gaps
- Current (Nov 2026) enforcement data, such as the number of listings blocked, was not found.

## Revenue breakdown, super-app strategy and traffic

### Takeaway
FY2025 revenue was $2.583B, up 16%. Residential (mostly agent advertising and referrals) was $1.704B, or 66%. Rentals was $630M (24%, up 39%), Mortgages $199M (up 37%), Other $50M. In 2026 Rentals and Mortgages keep growing fast while Residential softened (Q3 2026 -4%). Traffic is about 215-221M average monthly unique users, and Zillow holds about 77% of the US real-estate category audience (Comscore).

### Inferences

## Other monetization: new construction, Showcase, rentals, display advertising

### Takeaway
Beyond agent leads, Zillow sells:
- builder promotion (the New Construction marketplace, a cited growth driver)
- Showcase premium listings, paid by listing agents, on 3.7% of new US listings in Q4 2025
- multifamily rental advertising and pay-per-lease to property managers
- tools for small landlords through Zillow Rental Manager

Display advertising is a minor, undisclosed line inside "Other."

### Cited Findings
- **Showcase:** a premium listing with rich media, interactive floor plans, 3D tours, virtual staging and SkyTour. — [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1617640/000161764026000015/z-20251231.htm)
  - Share of new US listings: 3.2% at end of Q3 2025 (from 2.5% in Q2, more than double a year earlier). — [Q3 2025 call](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2025/10/30/zillow-z-q3-2025-earnings-call-transcript)
  - 3.7% in Q4 2025, versus 1.7% a year earlier. — [Q4 2025 letter (MarketScreener)](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/zillow-fourth-quarter-2025-shareholder-letter-ce7e5adcdf8bf221)
- **New Construction:** connects new-construction buyers with builder partners. Cited as a driver of Residential growth in Q3 and Q4 2025. — [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1617640/000161764026000015/z-20251231.htm); [Q4 2025 release](https://zillowgroup.mediaroom.com/2026-02-10-Zillow-Group-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2025-Financial-Results)
- **Rentals:**
  - Multifamily property managers buy advertising to fill vacancies.
  - Longtail landlords (fewer than 25 units) use Zillow Rental Manager for listing, advertising, leasing and property management.
  - Performance models include Pay-Per-Lease and StreetEasy Experts.
  - Source: [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1617640/000161764026000015/z-20251231.htm)
- **Rental Manager listing fees:** sources conflict.
  - Many landlord blogs cite $9.99 per listing per week.
  - Another source says standard listings have been free since May 2022, with optional Premium at $39.99 per listing for up to 90 days.
  - Screening fees (about $35 per application) are paid by applicants.
  - Sources: [DoorLoop](https://doorloop.com/blog/zillow-rental-manager-review); [rfp.wiki](https://www.rfp.wiki/specialty-industries/real-estate-property/zillow-rental-manager/apartments-com-rental-manager)
- **Redfin rentals deal and FTC suit:** Zillow paid Redfin $100M (from Feb 2025) to become the exclusive multifamily listings provider on Redfin, Rent.com and ApartmentGuide. The term is 5 years plus two 2-year extensions. The FTC sued on Sept 30, 2025, alleging an illegal agreement. Litigation costs appear in Q3 2026 results ($34M YTD). — [Inman](https://www.inman.com/2025/09/30/ftc-sues-zillow-redfin-over-100m-rental-syndication-deal/); [HousingWire](https://www.housingwire.com/articles/ftc-lawsuit-claims-zillow-redfin-rental-tie-up-crushes-competition/); [Q3 2026 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001617640/000161764026000068/q32026991.htm)

### Inferences
- Premium listing upgrades (Showcase) and builder/developer promotion are low-complexity revenue lines that map well to Kenya's developer-driven off-plan market.
- Zillow's use of free listings for small landlords, while charging property managers and premium placement, is a supply-acquisition tactic worth copying.

### Gaps
- No disclosed revenue for display advertising, Showcase or New Construction.
- Showcase pricing to agents was not found. It is reportedly priced per listing, but I did not verify this.

## Network effects and growth loops: SEO, Zestimate, alerts

### Takeaway
Zillow's dominance comes from a self-reinforcing loop: the most listings attract the most buyers, which makes it the best place for agents to advertise, which funds more product. Zillow holds about 77% of the US real-estate category audience. Traffic is mostly direct or app-based, with organic search second. The Zestimate drives homeowner and off-market engagement, but I found little 2025-2026 primary data on SEO or alert mechanics.

### Cited Findings
- **Category share:** Zillow had 77% of average monthly unique visitors in the real-estate category in Q3 2026 (Comscore). — [Q3 2026 8-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001617640/000161764026000068/q32026991.htm)
- **Hard to unseat:** DelPrete (Aug 2023) argues Zillow will be very hard to dethrone. Any competitor with less traffic is a less valuable advertising platform for agents. — [Inman](https://www.inman.com/2023/08/02/even-costar-doesnt-think-it-will-unseat-zillow-delprete-argues/)
- **Traffic sources:** third-party estimates (Similarweb/Semrush-based) put direct at about 55-57% and organic search at about 35-40%. Zillow has said most traffic comes direct. — [Zillow statistics roundup](https://www.rubyhome.com/blog/zillow-stats/); [Priceonomics](https://priceonomics.com/?p=6737); [Semrush](https://www.semrush.com/website/zillow.com/overview/)
- **Web traffic share (Similarweb):** 53% in March 2019, 49% in March 2022. — [Similarweb](https://similarweb.com/amp/blog/insights/zillow-redfin)
- **Zestimate accuracy (2025):** median error of 1.8% for on-market homes and 7.2% for off-market homes. Zillow provides valuations for off-market homes, which keeps owners engaged. — [10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1617640/000161764026000015/z-20251231.htm)
- **Google risk:** Google's real-estate listings experiment sent Zillow shares down more than 8%. — [GeekWire tag page](https://www.geekwire.com/tag/mike-delprete/)

### Inferences
- The levers are: a page for every property, location and neighborhood (programmatic SEO), a valuation hook for owners (owner dashboards), saved searches and email or push alerts, and an app that drives direct return visits. At Zillow's scale direct traffic dominates, but a new Kenyan portal will depend on SEO and social first.
- A "Keja estimate" needs transaction data that Kenya lacks. A rent-estimate based on listing data is more feasible.

### Gaps
- No primary 2025-2026 data on Zillow's programmatic SEO page counts, email-alert volumes, "claim your home" owner engagement, or app-versus-web share. Third-party traffic-mix estimates conflict.
