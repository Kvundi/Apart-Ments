# Zillow Rentals (renter + landlord sides) and Trust, Safety & Anti-Fraud (2025-2026)

Research method note: zillow.com, zillow.zendesk.com (help center) and zillow.com/rentals-network pages returned HTTP 403 to direct fetches, so Zillow's own help/marketing pages are cited through search-result excerpts of those pages. Third-party pricing blogs conflict heavily (several are low-quality SEO/content-farm pages; those were excluded). Where a figure rests on secondary sources it is flagged.

## 1. Renter side: search, listing page, contact/application, screening, payments, profiles, protections

### Takeaway
Zillow's renter funnel is: rental-specific search filters, then a listing page with rental-specific modules (total monthly price, costs & fees, local legal protections), then contact via in-app message / "Book Tour" / "Apply Now", then one reusable application ($35, renter-paid, valid 30 days across participating listings), then online rent payment with free credit-bureau reporting. The renter pays for screening; the landlord pays nothing for screening or rent collection.

### Cited Findings
**Search filters**
- App-store descriptions list filters for size, amenities, pet-friendly homes, in-unit laundry, on-site parking, drive time, and a "days on Zillow" filter to surface new listings; move-in-date filtering dates to 2022; saved-search alerts; multi-location search (up to 5 areas per 2022 coverage); nearby amenity overlays (grocery, parks, restaurants) — [Zillow Rentals, App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/zillow-rentals/id538946076); [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.zillow.android.rentals&hl=en-US); [OnlineMarketplaces, 2022 feature launch](https://www.onlinemarketplaces.com/articles/zillow-launches-new-features-could-save-users-thousands/)
- "Income Restricted" is reported as a filter for buildings with tax-credit-limited rents for households below a percentage of Area Median Income (secondary-source description; not confirmed on a Zillow page) — search excerpt via [Zillow rentals-network: source of income page](https://www.zillow.com/rentals-network/discrimination-income-sources)
- No dedicated "accepts Section 8 / vouchers" filter was found; instead each rental listing has a "Local Legal Protections" module telling renters whether the locale bans source-of-income discrimination — [Zillow: Voucher-Assistance Programs and Protections for Tenants](https://www.zillow.com/rentals-network/discrimination-income-sources)

**Listing page differences vs for-sale**
- "Total price" badge (base rent + required monthly fees; excludes usage-based and optional fees) shown across search results, detail pages, saved listings and emails; landlords could toggle it on from 25 Aug 2025; a separate "Costs & Fees" breakdown lists optional/variable charges. Driven by state laws: Massachusetts (2 Sep 2025), Connecticut (Oct 2025), Colorado (Jan 2026) — [Zillow: Understanding Zillow's New Total Price Display](https://www.zillow.com/rentals-network/pricing-transparency); [Zillow: Fee Transparency regulations](https://www.zillow.com/rentals-network/fee-transparency-regulations)
- Predecessor: July 2023 "Cost of Renting Summary" launched as part of the White House junk-fee initiative — [GeekWire, 2023](https://www.geekwire.com/2023/zillow-releases-tool-for-transparent-rental-pricing-as-white-house-targets-junk-fees/); [Zillow Group newsroom](https://www.zillowgroup.com/news/transparent-rental-pricing-for-renters-and-housing-providers)
- "Local Legal Protections" module on every rental listing (see above).
- Since May 2026, free rental listings no longer show the landlord's phone number; renters are routed to "Book Tour" / "Apply Now" / email / in-app messaging. Paid listings (Feed Connect, multifamily advertising contracts, premium Rental Manager upgrades) still show phone numbers. Zillow's stated rationale: "a proactive safety measure", citing AI tools making contact harvesting for spam/fraud easier — [Peter Lohmann blog citing RentEngine (7 May 2026) and a Zillow spokesperson](https://www.peterlohmann.com/blog/zillow-removes-phone-numbers-rental-listings) (landlord-critic source; argues it is a paywall)

**Application + screening**
- Single reusable application: renter pays $35, which covers unlimited applications to participating Zillow rentals for 30 days; landlords screen and collect rent for free — search excerpt of [Zillow Rental Manager help / RentSpree review](https://www.rentspree.com/blog/zillow-rental-manager-review); [Reallyo 2026 cost guide](https://www.reallyo.com/blog/zillow-rental-manager-cost)
- Conflict: older third-party sources give $29 per applicant; one aggregator cites "$32 plus tax per application as of a July 2026 help article" — [DoorLoop review](https://www.doorloop.com/blog/zillow-rental-manager-review); [Landlord Gurus](https://landlordgurus.com/zillow-rental-manager-review-software-overview/). Treat $35 as most commonly cited current figure, but unverified against Zillow directly.
- Landlord-side criticism of the Zillow application (e.g. NC/SC property manager) exists — [Carolina Property Management](https://www.carolinapropertymanagement.com/blog/why-zillows-rental-application-can-be-a-nightmare-for-landlords-in-nc-and-sc)

**Renter profile**
- Renter Profile lets renters pre-fill info to respond quickly and "stand out to landlords"; 2022 plans to add qualifications, move-in date and amenity preferences (shipping not confirmed) — [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.zillow.android.rentals&hl=en-US); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/zillow-rentals/id538946076)

**Online rent payments + credit building**
- Renters can pay online, set recurring payments and reminders through Rental Manager. Fees reported (secondary): ACH free, debit card $9.95, credit card 2.95% — [Mashvisor](https://www.mashvisor.com/blog/zillow-rental-manager/) (older source)
- Free rent reporting (since 2024) for renters who pay through Zillow, to Experian and Equifax; >1.78 million on-time payments reported as of Aug 2026; 78,000+ renters reported in July 2026 alone — [RISMedia, 31 Aug 2026](https://www.rismedia.com/2026/08/31/zillow-reports-1-78-million-rent-payments-to-credit-bureaus-through-rent-reporting-program/); [Zillow IR release 2026](https://investors.zillowgroup.com/news-and-events/news/news-details/2026/Zillow-is-helping-78000-renters-and-counting-turn-monthly-bills-into-credit-progress/default.aspx)
- CreditClimb (launched 19 Nov 2025, with Esusu): renters report rent to all three bureaus for $20/year even if they do not pay through Zillow; Zillow cites avg +45 points (later releases cite +53) — [Zillow press release](https://zillow.mediaroom.com/2025-11-19-Zillow-launches-CreditClimb-to-help-renters-build-credit-with-every-rent-payment); [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/consumer-finance/2025/zillow-lets-renters-report-payments-to-credit-bureaus/)

**Verified badges for renters**
- A "Verified Source" badge exists, but as a paid advertiser feature (included in multifamily Base/essentials package), not as a universal verification of every listing — [Zillow Multifamily Knowledge Center: Rent Connect packages](https://www.zillow.com/multifamily-knowledge-center/property-management/general-marketing/rent-connect-advertising-packages/); [Zillow Rental Advertising Products](https://www.zillow.com/rentals-network/rental-advertising/)

### Inferences
- Zillow monetizes the renter side directly only via the application fee and CreditClimb; its main value is lead volume that it sells to landlords.
- The "Total price" and "Local Legal Protections" modules are regulation-driven; a Kenyan analogue would be showing service charge, deposit months, and water/garbage fees up front.
- Removing phone numbers from free listings is simultaneously an anti-fraud and monetization lever (forces on-platform messaging where fraud is detectable; phone visibility becomes a paid perk).

### Gaps
- Could not confirm the current exact Zillow filter menu (e.g. whether "Income Restricted", "Accepts Zillow applications", "Short-term" are live filters) because zillow.com help pages were blocked.
- No primary source for current tenant-screening report contents (which bureau; credit + criminal + eviction) in 2025-2026.
- No official "renter protection" guarantee (e.g. deposit refund guarantee) was found; Zillow appears not to offer one.

## 2. Landlord / property-manager side: Rental Manager, fees, tools, paid products, network

### Takeaway
Small landlords list free in Zillow Rental Manager (since May 2022) with syndication to Zillow, Trulia and HotPads, plus an optional ~$39.99 premium upgrade for 90 days; leasing tools (applications, screening, lease, rent collection, messaging) are free to landlords and paid by renters. Professional/multifamily operators pay: Feed Connect (per-door syndication), Rent Connect (flat-rate packages), and Lease Connect (pay-per-lease). The 2025 Zillow-Redfin syndication deal extended Zillow's network to Redfin, Rent.com and ApartmentGuide; it was challenged by the FTC and settled in 2026.

### Cited Findings
**Listing fees (small landlords)**
- Zillow help center (via excerpt): as of May 2022, all listings posted directly to Rental Manager are free; same listing appears on Zillow, Trulia, HotPads — [Zillow Help: Creating a Listing](https://zillow.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/32915356424595-Creating-a-Listing); [Reallyo 2026](https://www.reallyo.com/blog/zillow-rental-manager-cost)
- History: Jan 2021 Zillow started charging $9.99/week per rental listing (first listing free), borrowed from StreetEasy — [The Real Deal, 19 Jan 2021](https://therealdeal.com/national/2021/01/19/zillow-is-now-charging-for-rental-listings/); [Hemlane](https://www.hemlane.com/resources/zillow-is-charging-for-rentals/). DISCONTINUED for direct Rental Manager listings in May 2022 per Zillow help excerpts; some 2026 blogs (e.g. DoorLoop) still repeat the $9.99/week claim, which conflicts with Zillow's help text.
- Premium upgrade: one-time fee for up to 90 days, does not auto-renew. Price conflict: $39.99 ([Reallyo 2026](https://www.reallyo.com/blog/zillow-rental-manager-cost)) vs $29.99 in older reviews ([DoorLoop](https://www.doorloop.com/blog/zillow-rental-manager-review)). $39.99 appears to be the current figure.
- Professional property managers/larger landlords must pay for feed syndication (Feed Connect, per-door rates varying by market); individual owners still post free — [Peter Lohmann](https://www.peterlohmann.com/blog/zillow-removes-phone-numbers-rental-listings)

**Listing creation + tools**
- Rental Manager bundles listing, applications/screening, leases, rent collection and messaging; landlords pay nothing for screening/rent collection — [RentSpree review](https://www.rentspree.com/blog/zillow-rental-manager-review); [Landlord Studio review](https://www.landlordstudio.com/blog/zillow-rental-manager-review)
- Premium perks and paid tiers include verified badges, premium placement, weekly performance reports, and phone number display (after May 2026) — [Peter Lohmann](https://www.peterlohmann.com/blog/zillow-removes-phone-numbers-rental-listings)

**Multifamily paid products**
- Billing models: Flat Rate (Rent Connect packages) or Pay-Per-Lease (Lease Connect) — [Zillow Rental Advertising Products](https://www.zillow.com/rentals-network/rental-advertising/); [Rent Connect](https://www.zillow.com/z/rental-property-advertising/rent-connect/)
- Lease Connect: charged only when a lease is signed and Zillow was the first point of contact; no charge when fully occupied — [Zillow Lease Connect](https://www.zillow.com/rentals-network/lease-connect/); [Multifamily Knowledge Center](https://www.zillow.com/multifamily-knowledge-center/property-management/lease-connect-tech-pay-per-lease-advertising/)
- Rent Connect Base Package includes a "Verified Source" badge — [Rent Connect packages](https://www.zillow.com/multifamily-knowledge-center/property-management/general-marketing/rent-connect-advertising-packages/)
- No public per-lease dollar price found.

**Zillow-Redfin rentals deal (2025) and FTC case**
- Feb 2025: Zillow paid Redfin $100M; Redfin exited rental-listing advertising (for up to nine years) and syndicated Zillow's multifamily listings — [Multifamily Dive](https://www.multifamilydive.com/news/zillow-redfin-ftc-lawsuit-settlement/); [Inman](https://www.inman.com/2025/09/30/ftc-sues-zillow-redfin-over-100m-rental-syndication-deal/)
- FTC sued 30 Sep 2025 (Sherman + Clayton Acts; alleged Zillow paid Redfin to stop advertising complexes of 25+ units); AZ, CT, NY, VA, WA sued 1 Oct 2025; cases combined Nov 2025; motion to dismiss denied May 2026 — [Real Estate News](https://www.realestatenews.com/2025/09/30/ftc-sues-zillow-and-redfin-over-rentals-deal); [Real Estate News, states join](https://www.realestatenews.com/2025/10/01/5-states-join-ftc-in-suing-zillow-redfin-over-rentals-partnership); [Multifamily Dive](https://www.multifamilydive.com/news/zillow-redfin-ftc-lawsuit-settlement/)
- Settlement (2026, no admission of liability): Redfin must re-enter the rental ILS market within six months of final order (expected 2027) with dedicated staff; Zillow must waive non-competes/anti-poaching, let locked-in ILS customers renegotiate penalty-free for nine months, not block customers contracting with Redfin; both must notify FTC before restrictive multifamily syndication deals. Syndication across Zillow, Trulia, HotPads, Rent.com, ApartmentGuide and Redfin continues "through at least 2030"; both will sell standalone multifamily advertising in 2027 — [Multifamily Dive](https://www.multifamilydive.com/news/zillow-redfin-ftc-lawsuit-settlement/). Note: exact settlement date not confirmed; a July 2026 Propmodo piece described an August trial, so settlement is mid/late 2026.

### Inferences
- The freemium split (individual landlords free, professionals pay per door / per lease) is a direct template for Kenya: free for individual owners, paid for agents/property managers and developers.
- Pay-per-lease requires reliable attribution ("first point of contact") — needs on-platform messaging/lead tracking.
- Moving free-tier contact behind on-platform messaging drives both fraud control and upgrades.

### Gaps
- Exact listing form fields and photo requirements (min/max photos, resolution) could not be retrieved from Zillow help pages (403). No reliable source found.
- Lease builder specifics (state-specific templates, e-sign, fee) not confirmed in 2025-2026 sources.
- Feed Connect per-door price not public.

## 3. Trust & safety: scam detection, landlord verification, reporting, warnings, fair housing, moderation

### Takeaway
Zillow's publicly documented anti-fraud stack is: automated screening of listings before/after posting (methods undisclosed), discretionary document/identity verification of listers via Persona, a "Report Listing" button, renter education pages, removal of phone numbers from free listings (2026), and fair-housing keyword filters. It does not verify every landlord before go-live. Most U.S. rental-scam losses originate off Zillow (Facebook ~50%).

### Cited Findings
- Zillow states it monitors site activity "in several different ways", screens for possible fraud to prevent posting, and removes fraudulent listings that get through as quickly as possible; users report via "Report Listing" on the listing page — [Zillow: How to spot rental scams](https://www.zillow.com/learn/how-to-spot-rental-scams) (excerpt via search; page blocked to direct fetch)
- Zillow red flags for renters: money requested before viewing, landlord "out of the country/remote", wire transfers/gift cards, requests for verification codes, requests for personal/financial info — [Zillow: How to spot rental scams](https://www.zillow.com/learn/how-to-spot-rental-scams)
- Landlord verification: Zillow may require Rental Manager listers to verify via a secure upload portal run by Persona (vendor may only use data to verify on Zillow's behalf); Zillow never charges to verify; verification requests only come via the Rental Manager dashboard, never by phone/text/listing message — [Zillow Help: Rental Manager Account Verification](https://zillow.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/40388811116179-Zillow-Rental-Manager-Account-Verification)
- Verification is discretionary: lister receives an email with a one-time link to the Persona portal when Zillow asks for proof — [FlagMyListing](https://flagmylisting.com/blog/zillow-rental-scams) (vendor of scam-check tools; moderate reliability)
- Critics: Zillow "doesn't fully verify every landlord's identity before a listing goes live" — [Incogni](https://blog.incogni.com/is-zillow-safe/); a property-manager blog claims Zillow does not independently verify uploaded documents (commercially interested, unconfirmed) — [Carolina Property Management](https://www.carolinapropertymanagement.com/blog/why-zillows-rental-application-can-be-a-nightmare-for-landlords-in-nc-and-sc)
- Scammers move conversations off-platform (WhatsApp, Telegram, personal email) to evade Zillow's fraud detection — [Incogni](https://blog.incogni.com/is-zillow-safe/)
- Common scam pattern: hijacking for-sale listings and reposting as rentals; a rental listing duplicating a for-sale property is "almost certainly fraudulent" — [Moonlock](https://moonlock.com/zillow-scams); FTC advice: same property listed with different prices/contact info is likely a scam — [FTC Rental Scams Data Spotlight 2025 (PDF)](https://search.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/rental-scams-spotlight-2025.pdf)
- May 2026: phone numbers removed from free listings as a "proactive safety measure" against AI-enabled harvesting for spam/fraud — [Peter Lohmann](https://www.peterlohmann.com/blog/zillow-removes-phone-numbers-rental-listings)
- Fair housing / content moderation: Zillow worked with the DC Attorney General to improve a filter across all its platforms that identifies and removes listing content with an expanded list of words/phrases associated with source-of-income discrimination (e.g. "no Section 8") — [DC OAG](https://oag.dc.gov/node/1650); [Zillow source-of-income page](https://www.zillow.com/rentals-network/discrimination-income-sources)
- Scale (U.S.): FTC Data Spotlight (Dec 2025) — ~65,000 rental-scam reports and ~$65M losses Jan 2020-Jun 2025, median loss $1,000; in 12 months to June 2025 about half started with a fake Facebook ad (Craigslist ~16% per secondary source); ages 18-29 three times more likely to report losses — [FTC Data Spotlight PDF](https://search.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/rental-scams-spotlight-2025.pdf); [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/fraud-attack/2025/rental-scams-cost-consumers-65-million-since-2020/)
- State AG warnings about fraudulent real-estate ads (e.g. Michigan AG, Mar 2024) — [Michigan AG](https://www.michigan.gov/ag/news/press-releases/2024/03/22/ag-nessel-warns-renters-homebuyers-of-fraudulent-real-estate-ads)

### Inferences
- The highest-leverage controls for Kenya: (1) detect duplicates of existing for-sale/rental listings with different contact details (photo hashing + address matching); (2) risk-triggered ID/ownership verification rather than universal KYC; (3) keep contact on-platform (masked numbers) so fraud is visible; (4) a clear "we will never ask you to pay to verify / never pay before viewing" message — especially relevant for M-Pesa "booking fee" scams.
- A paid "Verified" badge (as Zillow sells it) risks conflating payment with trust; a Kenyan site may prefer verification-based badges.

### Gaps
- No public data on Zillow's own fraud volumes (listings removed, % flagged) — not disclosed.
- Agent licensing verification for rentals: no 2025-2026 source found.
- Detection techniques (ML models, photo matching) are not publicly described by Zillow.

## 4. Listing quality standards: required info, photos, duplicates, expiry/staleness

### Takeaway
Free Rental Manager listings auto-expire unless the landlord confirms availability (reported: reminder around day 14, deactivation around day 21; 48-hour pre-expiry notice); premium listings run up to 90 days without auto-renew. "Days on Zillow" is exposed to renters as a filter/signal. Specific photo and field rules could not be verified.

### Cited Findings
- Free listings stay up ~21 days by default unless availability is confirmed; Zillow emails at ~day 14 and deactivates ~day 21 if unconfirmed; listings do not auto-renew; Zillow notifies 48 hours before expiry; listers can deactivate anytime — [Reallyo 2026 guide](https://www.reallyo.com/blog/how-to-post-a-rental-on-zillow); [Reallyo cost guide](https://www.reallyo.com/blog/zillow-rental-manager-cost) (secondary, consistent with Zillow help excerpts)
- Conflict: some sources describe a 30-day initial period — [DoorLoop](https://www.doorloop.com/blog/advertise-rental-property-free) (appears outdated)
- "Days on Zillow" filter available to renters for recency — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/zillow-rentals/id538946076)
- Total-price disclosure required in MA/CT/CO and optional elsewhere (see section 1) — [Zillow pricing transparency](https://www.zillow.com/rentals-network/pricing-transparency)
- Discriminatory wording filtered (see section 3) — [DC OAG](https://oag.dc.gov/node/1650)

### Inferences
- Short auto-expiry with availability confirmation is a cheap, effective staleness/fraud control for a Kenyan market where units fill fast and stale "bait" listings are common.

### Gaps
- Required fields and photo requirements (count, minimum resolution, watermark rules) not retrievable — Zillow help pages blocked; no reliable secondary source.
- No public documentation of Zillow's duplicate-detection logic for rentals.

## 5. Rentals segment revenue

### Takeaway
Rentals is Zillow's fastest-growing segment: $630M in FY2025 (+39% YoY from $453M), Q4 2025 $168M (+45%, multifamily +63%), Q1 2026 $183M (+42%); guidance ~30% growth for 2026.

### Cited Findings
- FY2025 Rentals revenue $630M vs $453M in 2024 (+39%); Q4 2025 $168M (+45%) driven by multifamily revenue +63%; 31M average monthly unique visitors to Zillow Rentals in Q4 2025; total Zillow FY2025 revenue $2.6B (+16%); 2026 outlook ~30% Rentals growth — [Zillow Group Q4/FY2025 press release, 10 Feb 2026](https://zillowgroup.mediaroom.com/2026-02-10-Zillow-Group-Reports-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2025-Financial-Results); [Q4 2025 shareholder letter via MarketScreener](https://www.marketscreener.com/news/zillow-fourth-quarter-2025-shareholder-letter-ce7e5adcdf8bf221)
- Q1 2026 Rentals revenue $183M, +42% YoY (HousingWire as cited) — [Peter Lohmann](https://www.peterlohmann.com/blog/zillow-removes-phone-numbers-rental-listings)

### Inferences
- Growth is driven by multifamily advertiser revenue (paid by professional operators), not small-landlord listing fees — consistent with free listings for individual owners.

### Gaps
- Q2/Q3 2026 Rentals figures not retrieved.
